Privacy Policy

The short version: Camipack has no account and keeps your trips in your own iCloud. If you choose to connect an AI assistant, a summary of the trips you share goes to Camipack's server. There are no analytics and no ads, and we never sell your data.

What data Camipack handles, and where it lives

Your trips — plans, places, routes, packing lists, expenses, and attached documents — are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, they also sync to your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), which belongs to your Apple account, not to us. When you share a trip with someone, that trip syncs through iCloud's shared database between the people you invited — and no one else.

The developer has no access to your iCloud data. The only time any trip data reaches a Camipack server is when you connect an AI assistant to a trip — see AI assistants (optional) below. If you never do, nothing about your trips is sent to us.

What Camipack does not do

  • No account or sign-up — the app never asks who you are.
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing of it for advertising.

Network connections the app makes

Camipack talks to these services:

  • Apple iCloud — to sync your trips between your devices and with the people you invite, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store — if you subscribe to Camipack Pro, Apple handles the purchase. We never see your name or payment details; the app only learns whether a subscription is active on your device.
  • Apple Maps — for map display, place search, and route directions, like any iOS maps app.
  • Protomaps CDN — only when you tap "Download Offline Map": the app fetches OpenStreetMap-based map tiles for your trip's area. Like any download, the server sees your IP address; no identifier, account, or trip content is sent with it.
  • Camipack AI server (ai.camipack.com) — only if you connect an AI assistant to a trip. Described in full below.

This website. The videos on the camipack.com home page come from YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you tap a video; then the player loads from youtube-nocookie.com, and Google's privacy policy applies to it.

AI assistants (optional)

You can connect an AI assistant — Claude, made by Anthropic, or ChatGPT, made by OpenAI — to one trip at a time, so it can read the plan and suggest or make changes. This is off until you turn it on for a trip, and you can turn it off at any time. Nothing in this section applies if you never connect an assistant.

How it works

When you connect a trip, the app shows an 8-character code. You sign in to your assistant, add Camipack to it and type the code. The app then uploads a summary of that trip to Camipack's server at ai.camipack.com, and the assistant reads it from there. If the assistant adds, moves or removes a stop, the change appears in the app straight away and you can undo it; if it proposes a day plan, nothing changes until you accept it in the app. There is no Camipack account: the app identifies itself with a random key kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

What is sent

For each trip you share with an assistant:

  • Trip name, dates and destinations.
  • Places and their times, areas and opening hours.
  • Notes on items, with names, phone numbers, email addresses, links and codes removed first.
  • Children's age bands, never their names or birth dates.
  • Pace and interests you chose for the trip.
  • Travel details: mode of travel, flight number, airports and times.
  • Stays: title, area and dates.
  • Documents: title and dates only.

Only if you switch them on for that trip: traveller names, street addresses, day notes, budget, and packing lists.

Never sent: map coordinates, booking codes, files and attachments, document notes, phone numbers, and email addresses.

From your device we also store an Apple push token (so we can tell you when the assistant changes your trip) and the app version.

Where it is stored, and who handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts Camipack's AI server and stores the data (Cloudflare Workers, D1 and KV), as our processor.
  • Apple delivers notifications such as "Claude changed 3 stops on Lisbon" through Apple Push Notifications.
  • The assistant you choose — Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT) — receives the trip summary when it reads your trip. What it does with it then is governed by its own terms and privacy policy, including how long it keeps your conversations: see Anthropic's privacy policy and OpenAI's privacy policy.

We do not sell this data or share it for advertising.

Why — legal basis

We process this data only because you asked us to, by connecting an assistant: the legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, and the equivalent under UK GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time, as described below; that does not affect what was done before you withdrew it.

International transfers

Cloudflare runs a global network, so the data may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States. Those transfers take place under Cloudflare's data processing terms, which include the safeguards required by data-protection law for such transfers.

How long it is kept

  • Trip summary: deleted when you stop sharing the trip with the assistant, or after 90 days in which it is not used.
  • Changes made by the assistant: deleted 30 days after they have been applied in the app.
  • Device records (push token, app version): deleted after 180 days in which the device has not been seen.
  • Assistant sign-in access: expires after 30 days at most unless the assistant renews it.

How to stop and delete

  • In the app: Settings → AI Assistants → Disconnect stops an assistant and removes its access.
  • Settings → AI Assistants → Delete everything on Camipack's server deletes all server data for your device at once.
  • Deleting the app does not by itself delete the server copy (it then expires on the schedule above). To delete it sooner, use the button above before you uninstall, or email privacy@camipack.com.
  • To remove Camipack from the assistant too, remove it from the assistant's own connector or plugin settings.

Your rights

You can ask for access to the data we hold, for its correction or deletion, and you can object to or restrict its processing. Email privacy@camipack.com. As there is no account, the quickest way to delete everything is the button in the app. You also have the right to complain to the data-protection authority where you live.

Permissions

Camipack asks for permissions only when you use the matching feature, and uses them only on the device: Calendar (to add your trip's events when you ask), Camera / Photos / Files (to attach documents you choose to a trip), and Notifications (for the alerts you turn on, such as when a connected assistant changes your trip). Denying a permission only disables that one feature.

Deleting your data

Delete a trip in the app and it is deleted from your device and from your iCloud sync. Delete the app to remove everything local. You can also remove Camipack's iCloud data at any time in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. If you connected an AI assistant, see How to stop and delete above for the server copy.

Children

Camipack is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. A parent can add children to a trip; if that trip is shared with an AI assistant, only the age band the parent chose is sent, never a child's name or birth date (unless the parent switches traveller names on). The app has no chat and no social features.

Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Privacy questions and requests: privacy@camipack.com. Anything else: support@camipack.com or open an issue on GitHub.

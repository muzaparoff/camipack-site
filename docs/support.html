Frequently asked

How do I plan a trip?

Tap + New Trip, name it, pick the destination and dates. Open the trip, tap any day, then tap the green + to add places, restaurants, walks, or tickets. Inside an activity, “Find on the map” pins it — numbered pins match your timeline order. The in-app Help (cloud pill → Help) has tap-by-tap guides for every feature.

How does sharing with family work?

Open the trip → ⋯ → Share Trip, and send the invitation (Messages works best). Once they join, you both edit the same trip and changes sync within moments. Both sides need to be signed in to iCloud. To stop: same menu → Stop Sharing.

How do offline maps work?

Open the trip → ⋯ → Offline Map… → Download Offline Map (a city is ~10–40 MB; use Wi-Fi). Then even in Airplane Mode, the trip map shows streets, labels, your pins and routes. If you change the plan later, tap Update Map to re-download around the new places.

Sync says “Sign in to iCloud” — what do I do?

Camipack syncs through your own iCloud. Go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud and make sure iCloud is on for Camipack. Without it the app still works fully on one device — you only lose sync and sharing.

Packing list changes aren’t showing on my family’s phone

Both phones need the current app version (older versions don’t know the newer record types). Check the App Store for updates on both devices, then pull down on the trip list to refresh.

Can I back up or move a trip?

Yes — open the trip → ⋯ → Export Trip. That creates a single .camipack file with the whole plan and its documents. Tapping such a file on any iPhone with Camipack offers to import it. Note an exported file is an independent copy — for live planning together, use Share Trip instead.

Can I read Camipack in my language?

Camipack is in English, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian. iOS lets you set the language for one app without changing your phone: iOS Settings → Camipack → Language. The app also links there from its own settings.

Where is my data stored? Do you have access to it?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We have no servers and no access — see the privacy policy.

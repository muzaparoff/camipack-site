Everything a family trip needs

🗺️

A living map, not a list

Every trip opens on a map that grows with your plan — pins tinted by day, walking routes drawn street by street. Plan a city, end up with a region? The map follows.

✈️

Offline maps

Download a trip's map once (a city is ~10–40 MB) and it works in Airplane Mode — streets, labels, your pins and routes. No signal abroad, no problem.

👨‍👩‍👧

Plan together

Share a trip with your family and edit it together — changes sync in moments through iCloud, with a little badge showing who changed what.

🎒

Packing, per person

A shared list plus one for every family member. Check things off and watch the trip's packed counter climb.

💶

Budget in any currency

Hotel in euros, museum in pounds, flights in shekels — every expense keeps its currency and the totals still make sense.

🎟️

Documents where you need them

Tickets and bookings attach to the day they belong to. Camipack reads them and fills in times and addresses for you.

📅

Calendar & widget

Put the whole trip in the family calendar with alerts, and keep the countdown — or today's route — on your Home Screen.

📦

Yours to keep

Export any trip as a single file — the full plan with documents. Back it up, send it, reimport it anywhere.

Privacy isn't a feature here — it's the architecture.
Camipack has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your trips live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and nowhere else. Read the two-minute privacy policy →

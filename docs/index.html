The trip planner that's actually yours.

Plan every day of a trip on a living map — places, walks, tickets, packing, budget and documents in one iPhone app. Everything stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. Nothing is collected. Ever.

Everything a trip needs

🗺️

A living map, not a list

Every trip opens on a map that grows with your plan — pins tinted by day, walking routes drawn street by street. Plan a city, end up with a region? The map follows.

✈️

Offline maps

Download a trip's map once (a city is ~10–40 MB) and it works in Airplane Mode — streets, labels, your pins and routes. No signal abroad, no problem.

👨‍👩‍👧

Plan together

Share a trip with the people you travel with and edit it together — changes sync in moments through iCloud, with a little badge showing who changed what.

🎒

Packing, per person

A shared list plus one for every traveller. Check things off and watch the trip's packed counter climb.

💶

Budget in any currency

Hotel in euros, museum in pounds, flights in shekels — every expense keeps its currency and the totals still make sense.

🎟️

Documents where you need them

Tickets and bookings attach to the day they belong to. With Camipack Pro, it reads them for you and fills in times and addresses.

📅

Calendar & widget

Put the whole trip in your calendar with alerts, and keep the countdown — or today's route — on your Home Screen.

📦

Yours to keep

Export any trip as a single file — the full plan with documents. Back it up, send it, reimport it anywhere.

Privacy isn't a feature here — it's the architecture.
Camipack has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your trips live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and nowhere else. Camipack Pro doesn't change that — no account to create, billed entirely through Apple. Read the two-minute privacy policy →

Use Camipack with ChatGPT (computer)

Camipack 1.151iPhone + computerUpdated 1 Nov 2026

ChatGPT, made by OpenAI, can read and change a Camipack trip too — but only on a computer, at chatgpt.com, as a plugin you add yourself. It does not work in the ChatGPT phone app, and whether you can add a plugin depends on your ChatGPT plan and settings.

What ChatGPT sees, and how its changes and day plans appear, is the same as for Claude — see Use Camipack with Claude.

Download on the App Store

  1. 1

    In Camipack, go to Settings → AI Assistants → Connect another assistant and pick ChatGPT. Choose the trip, agree, and keep the 8-character code on screen — it works once, for 10 minutes.

  2. 2

    On a computer, open chatgpt.com/plugins, click the + button, then Add custom MCP server.

  3. 3

    Name it Camipack. Under Connection, choose a public endpoint and enter https://ai.camipack.com/mcp.

  4. 4

    For authentication choose OAuth. Leave any client ID and client secret fields empty.

  5. 5

    Read the warning, click I understand and want to continue, then Create as a plugin.

  6. 6

    Click Connect. Camipack’s sign-in page opens: type the code from your iPhone and confirm.

  7. 7

    Start a new chat, type @Camipack, pick it, and ask about your trip.

Good to know

  • Works at chatgpt.com in a browser on a computer, and in the ChatGPT desktop app. Not in the ChatGPT phone app.
  • Adding your own plugin depends on your ChatGPT plan; on a work account your admin may need to allow it.
  • ChatGPT can be wrong. Check times, prices and opening hours before you rely on them.

Stop or delete

Remove the plugin at chatgpt.com/plugins, and in Camipack go to Settings → AI Assistants → Disconnect. Delete everything on Camipack’s server, on the same screen, removes all the data Camipack keeps for assistants at once. More in the privacy policy and on the Support page.

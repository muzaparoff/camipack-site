Add a trip to your calendar

Camipack 1.142iPhoneUpdated 24 Oct 2026

Put the trip into the calendar your family already checks: one all-day banner across the whole trip, and optionally every timed activity as its own event with an alert.

Download on the App Store

  1. 1

    Open the trip and tap the ⋯ button in the top-right corner.

  2. 2

    Tap “Share & Export”, then “Add to Calendar…”.

  3. 3

    Choose what to add — the whole trip, every timed item, or both — and pick the alerts.

  4. 4

    Pick the calendar and tap “Add”. Re-adding later updates the events instead of duplicating them.

Guide · 4 steps · 1 min

Home Screen widget

Camipack 1.142iPhoneUpdated 24 Oct 2026

The widget shows your next trip with its photo, countdown, and — during the trip — today's route. Tapping it opens that trip directly.

Download on the App Store

  1. 1

    Long-press an empty spot on your iPhone Home Screen until the apps wiggle.

  2. 2

    Tap Edit → “Add Widget”, then search for Camipack.

  3. 3

    Pick the small or medium size and tap “Add Widget”.

  4. 4

    From now on, one tap on the widget jumps straight into the trip it shows.
